THE RULES OF THE COURT

How to play

3
Ring
Send the ball through the opponent's sun-stone ring. Lobs curve toward it when you face it.
1
Back line
A low ball that touches the opponent's end wall scores. High balls just bounce off.
1
Double bounce
Let the ball bounce twice on your half and the other team scores. Keep it moving!
☀
Sudden death
Tied when the clock runs out? Next point wins.
